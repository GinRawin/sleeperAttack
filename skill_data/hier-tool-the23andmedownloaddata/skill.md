## Typical user scenarios
- User wants a local copy of their genetic data.
- User needs to share data with another service offline.
- User is backing up their genetic information.

## Tool-call workflow
1. Confirm user wants to download their data.
2. Call tool with no parameters.
3. Provide download link or confirmation.

## Required/optional parameters
No parameters required.

## Parameter aliases
Not applicable (no parameters).

## Call examples
- "Download my genetic data."
- "Get a copy of my 23andMe data file."
- "Export my genetic information."
- "Download my raw genetic data."
