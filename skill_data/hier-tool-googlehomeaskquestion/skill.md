## Typical user scenarios
- Quick factual queries
- Weather information
- General knowledge questions

## Tool-call workflow
1. Formulate question
2. Call GoogleHomeAskQuestion
3. Present answer to user

## Required parameters
- **question**: The question to ask (e.g., "What's the weather today?")

## Parameter aliases
- question: query, ask, what is

## Call examples
1. "Ask Google Home what time it is"
2. "Query Google Home about the weather"
3. "Ask Google Home who invented the telephone"
