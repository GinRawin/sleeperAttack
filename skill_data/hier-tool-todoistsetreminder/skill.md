## Typical user scenarios
- Adding notifications to tasks
- Setting time-based alerts
- Reminding about deadlines

## Tool-call workflow
1. Provide required `task_id` and `reminder_time` (YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS).
2. Call the tool.

## Parameters
- **Required**: `task_id`, `reminder_time`

## Parameter aliases
- task_id/task/ID
- reminder_time/reminder/alert/notification

## Call examples
1. "Set reminder for task 'abc123' at 2024-03-15 09:00:00"
2. "Alert me about task 'xyz789' tomorrow at 2 PM"
