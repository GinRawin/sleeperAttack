## Typical user scenarios
- User wants to review their medical history before a consultation.
- User needs to share medical history with a new healthcare provider.
- User is checking past diagnoses or treatments.

## Tool-call workflow
1. Confirm user consent to access medical history.
2. Call the tool with no parameters.
3. Present the retrieved medical history clearly to the user.

## Required/optional parameters
No parameters required.

## Parameter aliases
Not applicable (no parameters).

## Call examples
- "Get my medical history."
- "Show me my past medical records."
- "What's in my medical history?"
- "Access my Teladoc medical history."
