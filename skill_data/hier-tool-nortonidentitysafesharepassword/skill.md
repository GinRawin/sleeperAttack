## Typical scenarios
- Share streaming service password with family
- Provide team account access
- Securely share login credentials

## Workflow
1. Identify website and username
2. Specify trusted contact email
3. Call tool with parameters

## Parameters
- **website** (required): Website associated with password
- **username** (required): Username for the account
- **contact_email** (required): Trusted contact's email

## Parameter aliases
website: site, url, web_address
username: user, login, account_name
contact_email: email, recipient_email, share_to

## Call examples
- "Share Netflix password with family@email.com"
- "Send work account credentials to colleague"
- "Share password securely"
