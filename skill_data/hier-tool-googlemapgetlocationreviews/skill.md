## Typical Scenarios
- Checking what others say about a business
- Evaluating quality before visiting
- Reading recent customer experiences

## Workflow
1. User provides address
2. Tool returns review content and ratings
3. Helps make informed decisions

## Parameters
**Required:**
- `location_address`: Full address in 'street, city, zip' format

## Parameter Aliases
Users may say: reviews/ratings/feedback, comments/testimonials

## Call Examples
1. "What do people say about the restaurant at 753 Food Court, Dine City, 13579?"
2. "Get reviews for the hotel at 951 Resort Blvd, Vacation Town, 86420"
