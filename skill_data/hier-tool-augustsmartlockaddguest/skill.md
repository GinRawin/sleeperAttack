## Typical user scenarios
- Adding a house sitter or cleaner
- Granting temporary access to contractors
- Setting up access for family members

## Tool-call workflow
1. Collect guest name and email
2. Call AugustSmartLockAddGuest
3. Note the returned guest ID for future access management

## Required parameters
- **guest_name**: Full name of the guest (e.g., "John Smith")
- **guest_email**: Valid email address (e.g., "john@example.com")

## Parameter aliases
- guest_name: name, full name, person
- guest_email: email, email address, contact email

## Call examples
1. "Add guest Sarah Johnson with email sarah@email.com"
2. "Create guest entry for Mike Wilson at mike.wilson@company.com"
3. "Add new guest: Tom Brown, tom@brownfamily.net"
