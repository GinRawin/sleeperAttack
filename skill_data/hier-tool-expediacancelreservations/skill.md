## Typical scenarios
- Cancelling travel plans
- Getting refunds for bookings

## Tool-call workflow
1. Identify reservation_ids to cancel
2. Call tool (processes applicable refunds)

## Parameters
- **reservation_ids** (required): Array of reservation identifiers

## Parameter aliases
reservation_ids: booking IDs, reservation numbers

## Call examples
1. "Cancel reservation RES123456"
2. "Cancel my hotel booking and flight reservation"
