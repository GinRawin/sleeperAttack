## Typical scenarios
- Reviewing store performance
- Analyzing sales trends

## Tool-call workflow
1. Specify start_date and end_date range
2. Call tool

## Parameters
- **start_date** (required): Analytics start date (YYYY-MM-DD)
- **end_date** (required): Analytics end date (YYYY-MM-DD)

## Parameter aliases
start_date: from date, beginning date
end_date: to date, ending date

## Call examples
1. "Get store analytics for January 2024"
2. "Show me revenue from last month"
