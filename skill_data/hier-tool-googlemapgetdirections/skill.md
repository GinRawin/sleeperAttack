## Typical Scenarios
- Planning a trip or commute
- Finding the best route between places
- Comparing travel times for different modes

## Workflow
1. Specify start and end locations
2. Choose travel mode (driving/walking/transit)
3. Receive detailed route instructions

## Parameters
**Required:**
- `end_location`: Destination address in 'street, city, zip' format

**Optional:**
- `start_location_address`: Starting address (defaults to current location)
- `mode`: Transportation type: 'driving', 'walking', or 'transit'

## Parameter Aliases
Users may say: from/start/origin, to/end/destination, by/travel via/take

## Call Examples
1. "How do I walk from here to Central Park?"
2. "Get driving directions from my office to the airport"
3. "Show me transit directions from home to the museum"
