## Typical scenarios
- Review existing policy configurations
- Audit policy settings before modifications
- Check policy details for troubleshooting

## Workflow
1. Obtain policy ID
2. Call tool with policy_id

## Parameters
- **policy_id** (required): Unique policy identifier

## Parameter aliases
policy_id: id, policy identifier, policy number

- "Show details for policy POL-12345"
- "Get information about marketing policy"
