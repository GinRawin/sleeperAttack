## Typical Scenarios
- Removing outdated content
- Deleting accidental posts
- Cleaning up old posts

## Workflow
1. Confirm post deletion is intended
2. Locate exact post_id
3. Call tool with post_id

## Parameters
**Required:**
- `post_id`: Exact Facebook post identifier

**Aliases:** post_id → post/ID

## Examples
1. "Delete post 12345"
2. "Remove my post with ID ABCDE"
3. "Take down Facebook post XYZ"
