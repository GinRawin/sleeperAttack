**Typical Scenarios:**
- Review stock positions
- Check portfolio diversification
- Monitor investment performance

**Workflow:**
1. Specify account type
2. Retrieve holdings details

**Parameters:**
- account (required): 'self-directed TFSA' or 'self-directed non-registered'

**Parameter Aliases:**
- account: account_type/type/acct

**Call Examples:**
1. "Show my self-directed TFSA holdings" → account: 'self-directed TFSA'
2. "What stocks do I own in non-registered?" → account: 'self-directed non-registered'
3. "List my brokerage positions" → account: 'self-directed TFSA'
