## Typical scenarios
- Checking specific product details
- Verifying stock before sale
- Reviewing item specifications

## Tool-call workflow
1. Obtain item_id from inventory listing or search
2. Call tool with item_id
3. Review returned item information

## Parameters
**Required:**
- item_id: Unique item identifier

## Parameter aliases
- item_id: product_id/stock_id/item_number

## Call examples
1. "Show details for item INV-456"
2. "Get information about Product X"
3. "View details of item 789"
4. "Check specifications for widget"
